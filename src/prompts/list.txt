PhD-Level Topics in Neuroscience, Therapy, and Technology
1. Computational Neuroscience & AI-Driven Neurorehabilitation
Title: Neuroplasticity Modulation via Reinforcement Learning Algorithms in Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) for Post-Stroke Motor Recovery

Investigate the role of Hebbian learning and spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP) in AI-driven neurostimulation for post-stroke motor rehabilitation.
Explore how recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and transformer-based architectures optimize proprioceptive feedback loops in closed-loop BCI therapy.
Assess whether AI-generated reinforcement learning models can surpass traditional physiotherapy in inducing long-term synaptic potentiation in motor cortex circuits.
2. Neuromodulation & Personalized Therapy
Title: Individualized Transcranial Focused Ultrasound (tFUS) for Targeted Thalamocortical Dysrhythmia in Treatment-Resistant Depression (TRD)

Develop a patient-specific computational model integrating diffusion tensor imaging (DTI) and resting-state fMRI to optimize neuromodulation targeting strategies.
Examine the neuromechanics of tFUS-induced neuromodulation on the anterior cingulate cortex (ACC) and dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) in TRD.
Investigate neurophysiological markers (e.g., altered beta oscillations, theta-gamma coupling) that predict treatment efficacy in a deep-learning framework.
3. Neuroethics & AI-Psychotherapy
Title: Autonomous Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Agents: Ethical and Neural Implications of AI-Driven Emotional Regulation Training

Analyze real-time EEG neurofeedback integration in AI-driven CBT models, particularly in enhancing prefrontal-limbic connectivity for emotion regulation.
Assess the risk of algorithmic bias in AI-driven therapy due to data limitations, particularly in reinforcement learning models predicting affective dysregulation.
Explore the implications of digital therapeutics on long-term neuroplasticity and the potential for AI-induced maladaptive cognitive restructuring.
4. Neurosecurity & AI
Title: Brain Signal Cryptography: Adversarial Risks in BCI-Based Authentication Systems Using EEG Biometrics

Evaluate the vulnerability of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and graph neural networks (GNNs) in classifying EEG-based authentication signatures.
Investigate adversarial perturbation attacks on steady-state visual evoked potentials (SSVEP)-based authentication models.
Develop a zero-trust security framework for brainwave authentication, integrating homomorphic encryption and federated learning to prevent neurohacking.
5. Psychedelic Neuroscience & AI-Enhanced Therapy
Title: AI-Augmented Analysis of Psychedelic-Induced Brain Network Reconfiguration in Psilocybin-Assisted Therapy for Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)

Leverage deep-learning-driven dynamic causal modeling (DCM) to map psilocybin-induced entropy changes in large-scale brain networks.
Investigate how AI-assisted real-time fMRI neurofeedback enhances integration of psychedelic experiences for long-term therapeutic efficacy.
Explore how psychedelics disrupt the default mode network (DMN) and whether AI-driven predictive coding models can quantify therapeutic potential.


The Ethical and Neurological Implications of AI-Driven Neurotherapy
    Neuroscience: How AI-powered brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) and neurostimulation techniques (e.g., transcranial magnetic stimulation, deep brain stimulation) affect neuroplasticity, cognitive function, and long-term neural health.
    Therapy: The risks of over-reliance on AI-driven mental health therapy, potential bias in AI-generated treatment plans, and how human therapists can integrate AI without losing the therapeutic alliance.
    Technology: The security, privacy, and ethical dilemmas of AI interpreting brain signals, potential for manipulation, and whether AI should have decision-making power in treatment plans.
